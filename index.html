    About

    Hi, I'm Giu c:

    Brazilian visual artist born and raised in São Paulo. I've been drawing since I was a kid — it started with creatures, fueled by films and games like StarCraft and World of Warcraft, and later grew into a deep love for human anatomy.

    Self-taught, with a bachelor's degree in Visual Arts. These days I live in dark and high fantasy, character design and building YATHRA.

    “Giums” is short for my name — the full one is long and gets mispronounced a lot xD
